DLTECH Academy Abuja · Preschool to Grade 12

A JOURNEY OF DISCOVERY

Learning that prepares students for what comes next.

DLTECH Academy Abuja provides education from Preschool through Grade 12, with dedicated Early Childhood, Elementary, Middle School and High School divisions.

Our learning approach brings together the Nigerian and British curricula, with opportunities to explore ideas, apply knowledge and develop the confidence to take on new challenges. We place particular emphasis on digital literacy, science, technology, engineering and creative problem-solving.

OUR ACADEMIC FRAMEWORK

A strong foundation. A wider outlook.

Our academic programme combines established curriculum pathways with contemporary skills and global perspectives.

01

Nigerian & British Curriculum

A blended curriculum designed to develop a strong academic foundation and prepare students for local and international pathways.

02

Assessment & Examinations

Students prepare for relevant national and international examinations, including WAEC, NECO, IGCSE, SAT, A’ Level and AP examinations.

03

International Education

Global exchange programmes, technology-corridor internships and cross-border hackathons broaden students’ learning experiences.

04

Language & Digital Support

English as an Additional Language (EAL) support works alongside digital literacy to strengthen communication and technical writing.
