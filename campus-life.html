Life at DLTECH

More than a school.
A community.

At DLTECH Academy Abuja, campus life is an essential part of every learner’s journey. Students are encouraged to discover their strengths, build friendships, take on challenges and contribute positively to the world around them.

From student wellbeing and technology to sport, creative expression and outdoor learning, each experience helps students develop confidence, character and a sense of belonging.

Care comes first

Student well-being

Every student deserves to feel safe, supported and ready to learn. DLTECH Academy’s student support approach brings together counsellors, language specialists and special-needs specialists to support learners alongside their academic development.

Our goal is to recognise each learner’s needs and help every student participate, build resilience and make progress.
