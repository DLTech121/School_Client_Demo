WHERE IT ALL BEGAN

From vision to
innovation hub.

DLTech Academy Abuja is a futuristic, co-educational secondary school in Abuja, founded on 15 January 2017 to pioneer next-generation digital learning and technical excellence in Nigeria.

Established by a consortium of tech entrepreneurs and educators, DLTech began with a mission to bridge the gap between traditional African education and the rapidly evolving global digital landscape.

2017

The founding vision

Established by technology entrepreneurs and educators to connect African education with the global digital future.

2017

Campus milestone

The eco-friendly FCT campus opened, welcoming its first cohort of 150 students.

2025

Advanced technology wing

The academy commissioned its Robotics and Artificial Intelligence wing and developed partnerships for certified technology pathways.

2026

National recognition

DLTech strengthened its position as a hub for young innovators, earning recognition at national science exhibitions and technology hackathons.

OUR VISION FOR 2030

Preparing Africa's
next tech leaders.

DLTech Academy envisions becoming Africa’s premier incubator for young tech leaders by 2030, empowering students to engineer global solutions through technology mastery, ethical leadership and student-led venture acceleration.

OUR ACADEMIC IDENTITY

Education with a
future-facing edge.

We bring strong academic foundations together with practical technology pathways, encouraging students to create, question and solve.

01

Dual-Curriculum Fusion

Blending the Nigerian National Curriculum with advanced global STEM frameworks.

02

Industry Certifications

Embedding professional technology tracks such as AI, robotics and cloud computing into secondary education.

03

Project-Based Learning

Hands-on venture incubation, experimentation and product building beyond memorisation.

04

Global Readiness

Equipping graduates with academic credentials and a portfolio of technology projects and solutions.
